About Us

From Architecture to Furniture

CGLUX HOME combines European architectural and interior design expertise with professional furniture manufacturing in China.

We provide custom furniture solutions for villas, apartments, hotels and resorts, from design concepts to final furniture production.

Our team works closely with international architects, designers and clients to develop furniture that fits the space, style and functional requirements of each project.

From furniture sourcing and customization to factory production, quality follow-up, professional packing and international shipping, we coordinate the complete process.
